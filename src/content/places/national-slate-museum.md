The museum has the largest working waterwheel in mainland Britain, over 15 metres across, and a row of Victorian quarry workers' cottages moved here stone by stone from Tanygrisiau. The Llanberis Lake Railway runs from the same site.

- **Closed for a big redevelopment and not due to reopen until 2027.** Check the museum website before you go
- In the 19th-century workshops of the Dinorwic quarry, inside Padarn Country Park
- Part of Amgueddfa Cymru, the national museums of Wales
- Pair it with [Llanberis](/places/llanberis) and a drive around Snowdon
