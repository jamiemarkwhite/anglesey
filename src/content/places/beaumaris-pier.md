### Crabbing
- Best at high tide, so check the tide times below
- Buy equipment (~£12) near the pier or sweet shop
- Bait: cheap bacon from Spar works well
- Fill the bucket with seawater and add stones as weights

### Fishing
- A good fishing spot off the pier too

### Nearby
- [Beaumaris Castle](/places/beaumaris-castle) is a short walk away
- [Puffin Island cruises](/places/seacoast-safaris) depart from the pier
- Kids playground in town
