Anglesey Sea Zoo is an independent aquarium just outside Brynsiencyn, open since 1983. It says it suits all ages, from in-depth facts about marine wildlife for older children to a toddler play area. Moel-y-Don beach is less than a mile away if you want to add a beach stop.

- Over 40 tanks displaying 150+ native British marine species
- See octopus, lobsters, seahorses, conger eels and jellyfish
- Successful seahorse breeding programme
- Part of marine animal rescue network
- First UK aquarium powered by solar energy
- Crazy golf on site
- Play area and free bouncy castle (April–October)
- Café and refreshments
