Aydin's is a café and takeaway in Rhosneigr, open since 2011, a few minutes' walk from the beach. The menu is short, mostly pizzas and burgers.

- The **Rhosneigr Special** is the one to order
- Ring ahead and collect if you'd rather not wait (their number is on [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Aydin's+pizza/@53.2284711,-4.5213967,265m/data=!3m1!1e3!4m6!3m5!1s0x4865b42e2d13ea7b:0x3f869ba8941e9894!8m2!3d53.2286846!4d-4.5205519!16s%2Fg%2F1hc2439j4))
- Takes cards now. For a long time it was cash only
- Draught beer and other drinks too
- Opens about 5ish
