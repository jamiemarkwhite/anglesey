Llangefni takes its name from the River Cefni. [Oriel Môn](/places/oriel-mon), the island's museum and gallery, runs workshops for children and families, and [the Dingle](/places/the-dingle) is a 10-hectare wooded valley with a boardwalk along the river. There is an open-air market on Thursdays and Saturdays.
