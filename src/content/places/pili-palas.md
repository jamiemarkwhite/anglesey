Pili Palas Nature World is near Menai Bridge. Besides the butterfly house there are meerkats, lizards, a bug zone, pygmy goats and Kune Kune pigs. Outside there are climbing towers, zip wires and a short nature trail with a pond and bird hide. The indoor areas, café and shop are pram friendly, but the nature trail is not suitable for prams.

- Butterflies, birds, reptiles, insects and small animals
- Walk through the tropical house surrounded by free-flying butterflies
- Soft play area for children
- Great for rainy days
