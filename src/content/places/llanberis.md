### Drive around Snowdon
- From Llanberis, go up the Llanberis Pass (A4086) to Pen-y-Pass
- Turn right at Pen-y-Gwryd onto the A498, down to Beddgelert
- Head back north on the A4085 through Rhyd Ddu to Caernarfon

### In Llanberis
- On the shore of Llyn Padarn
- The Snowdon Mountain Railway runs from here to the summit
- The [National Slate Museum](/places/national-slate-museum) is here too
