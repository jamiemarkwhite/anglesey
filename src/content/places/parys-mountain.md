People have dug copper out of Parys Mountain for about 4,000 years. What's left is a bare, orange and purple landscape that looks nothing like the rest of the island.

### History

- Bronze Age miners worked here nearly 4,000 years ago
- A local miner, Rowland Pugh, found the Great Lode on 2 March 1768. He was given a bottle of whisky and a rent-free house for life
- In the 1780s it was the largest copper mine in Europe, and its copper dominated the world market
- Underground mining stopped in 1904

### The walk

- Two waymarked trails round the old workings, one long and one short
- The long loop is about 3 miles (4.8 km). Allow 1.5 to 2 hours
- The short loop takes under an hour
- Rocky, uneven ground with steep drops, so keep hold of small children
- Free to explore
- [See the incredible views](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Mynydd+Parys)

### Parking

- Free car park beside the B5111 road out of Amlwch

### Copper Kingdom

- The Copper Kingdom centre at Amlwch Port, by the harbour, tells the story of the mine and the people who worked it
