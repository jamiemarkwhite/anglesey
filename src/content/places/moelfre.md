A small fishing village on the east coast with a long lifeboat history.

Moelfre has had a lifeboat since 1848, and a memorial on the headland marks the wreck of the Royal Charter nearby in 1859. Seals and porpoises are sometimes seen around Ynys Moelfre, the island off the village, and the Anglesey Coastal Path leads north to the big sandy beach at Lligwy Bay.

### Lifeboat station
- [Moelfre RNLI Lifeboat Station](https://rnli.org/find-my-nearest/lifeboat-stations/moelfre-lifeboat-station) is a working station
- Viewing platform to watch the lifeboat and the sea
- 37 medals for gallantry in the station's history

### RNLI Seawatch Centre
- [Free museum](https://rnli.org/find-my-nearest/museums/moelfre-rnli-seawatch-centre) with a retired lifeboat
- Statue of the coxswain Richard "Dic" Evans outside
- Kids can dress up in crew kit

### Ann's Pantry
- [Ann's Pantry](https://www.annspantry.co.uk/) is a family-run café and restaurant in the middle of the village, close to the car parks
- Lovely for lunch
- Opening is seasonal, so ring ahead

### Also
- Small waterfall and tiny beach
- Pub with outdoor sea views
